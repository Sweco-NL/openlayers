--- layout: example.html title: Draw Curved Geometries shortdesc: Draw CircularStrings, CompoundCurves, and CurvePolygons. docs: > Draw curved geometries interactively.
  • CircularString - click 3+ points (odd count), double-click to finish.
  • CompoundCurve - draw a mix of arc and line segments. Press T to commit the current segment and toggle to the other type. Double-click to finish.
  • CurvePolygon - same as CompoundCurve, but the ring auto-closes. Click near the start point or double-click to finish.
  • None (edit) - no drawing. Drag vertices to modify, Alt+Click to delete, click an edge to insert.
tags: "draw, circularstring, compoundcurve, curvepolygon, curve, arc, interaction" ---
